01

Executive Summary

  • We generated ₹19.76 Lakhs in B2C revenue in August, which was 19.36% higher than August 2025 (16.5 lakhs), while spending 71.50% less than the previous year.
  • In the middle of the month, to further push revenue, we moved the available budget from the Experience Centre to B2C, as the Experience Centre had limited appointment slots and there was more room to scale B2C.
  • B2C generated ₹19.76 Lakhs in total revenue, 19.36% higher than last August, with 863 orders.
  • The Rakhi period generated 368 orders and ₹8.47 Lakhs in total sales. The UGC-led Rakhi creatives played an important role in supporting sales during the period and helped us generate strong traction through the campaign.
  • On B2B, we spent ₹41,673 and generated 118 leads at an average CPL of ₹353. Approximately 94 leads were qualified, resulting in a CPQL of ₹441, at a qualification rate of 80%.
  • While we did not achieve the B2B revenue target within August, the quality of leads coming through has been positive. Since the focus was primarily on generating demand for Diwali corporate gifting, some of these leads are expected to convert in September rather than within the same month.
  • We also tested offer-led B2B creatives, communicating that customers who pre-booked their Diwali corporate gifting requirements before the deadline would receive 10% off MRP.
  • Experience Centre generated ₹8.74 Lakhs in revenue against the target of ₹11.04 Lakhs. The specific EC campaigns were paused on 13 August based on the Smoor's request, as slots were limited and the team was also preparing for the Rakhi event.
  • We also ran Independence Day and Rakhi creatives developed by the creative team on Google, along with the influencer content shared by the client for Rakhi.
  • Overall, the strong performance from B2C helped the account generate ₹19.76 Lakhs in revenue, exceeding the monthly target of ₹15 Lakhs.

Overview Summary

What happened in each business vertical this month.

B2C

  • B2C generated ₹19.76L in revenue, 19.36% higher than August 2025 (₹16.5L), while spending 71.50% less than the same period last year.
  • In the middle of the month, we moved the available budget from Experience Centre to B2C, as EC had limited appointment slots and there was more room to scale B2C.
  • The Rakhi period generated 368 orders and ₹8.47L in total sales. The UGC-led Rakhi creatives played an important role in supporting sales during the period. We also ran Independence Day and Rakhi creatives developed by the creative team on Google, along with the influencer content shared by the client for Rakhi.
  • At the category level, Food Gift Baskets nearly tripled versus July (up 193.72%), moving up to the #2 category, largely on the back of the Memoire Tin and AURA HAMPER.

B2B

  • We spent ₹41,673 and generated 118 leads at an average CPL of ₹353. Approximately 94 leads were qualified, resulting in a CPQL of ₹441.
  • While we did not achieve the B2B revenue target within August, the quality of leads coming through has been positive. Since the focus was primarily on generating demand for Diwali corporate gifting, some of these leads are expected to convert in September rather than within the same month.
  • We also tested offer-led B2B creatives, communicating that customers who pre-booked their Diwali corporate gifting requirements before the deadline would receive 10% off MRP.
Best Performing Creatives

These two creatives were the best performing in B2B this month and brought in most of the leads.

"Choose hampers that look premium, feel thoughtful, and fit your budget." Corporate hamper offer creative, promoted on Meta and Instagram.
"Don't let last minute planning cost you." Diwali corporate gifting creative, driving quote requests via Meta and Instagram.

Experience Centre

  • Experience Centre generated ₹8.74 Lakhs in revenue against the target of ₹11.04 Lakhs. The specific EC campaigns were paused on 13 August based on Smoor's request, as slots were limited and the team was also preparing for the Rakhi event.
  • The budget freed up here is what we moved into B2C mid-month.
05

Recommendations & Areas for Improvement

  • Timely availability of fresh creatives will be important going forward, especially with the higher revenue target for September and the upcoming Diwali period.
  • Having more creatives available on time will allow us to test different messaging and formats and scale the ones that are working.
  • We need 5-6 influencer/UGC campaigns available through September, as the Rakhi campaign showed strong potential from this format.
  • For B2B, we should continue following up with the leads generated in August as well, as the lead quality has been positive and the longer consideration cycle means some of these enquiries are more likely to convert in September.
06

Targets & Action Items for September

Planned activity and targets for each vertical in September 2026.

  • The September media budget has been increased to ₹2 Lakhs, with ₹95,000 allocated to B2C, ₹80,000 to B2B and ₹25,000 to Experience Centre.
  • Based on the revised media plan, the projected revenue is ₹23.76 Lakhs from B2C, ₹12.60 Lakhs from B2B and ₹6.13 Lakhs from Experience Centre, giving a total projected revenue of ₹42.49 Lakhs. This target revenue is also subject to promoting the UGC/influencer activities efficiently.
  • B2B will primarily focus on Diwali corporate gifting, with the increased budget being used to build lead volume and capture demand ahead of the festive period. The spend has been scaled up to increase the volume and quality of leads, considering the upcoming festive season and high demand in Diwali corporate gifting. We're also running a separate campaign focused on Bangalore to bring in more Bangalore-concentrated leads.
  • The 10% off offer that was running on B2B has been removed. This could impact our ongoing B2B strategy and goal achievement going forward.
  • B2C will focus on influencer/UGC campaigns during the month, along with leveraging the Ganesh Pooja period and the Modak product to capture festive demand.
  • We're also testing a shipping charge benchmark reduction in B2C this month. Smoor previously offered free shipping on orders above ₹2,500; this has been reduced to ₹1,500, with the objective of bringing higher traction.
  • To achieve the projected B2C revenue target, it's important that the paid media plan is supported with a minimum of 5-6 UGC/Influencer videos. These creatives are a key driver of the additional revenue uplift we're targeting, as they'll provide fresh content for us to test, optimise, and scale throughout the month. We'd recommend having all the content ready before 15th September, as this gives us sufficient time in the second half of the month to identify the best-performing creatives and scale them effectively. Without this content support, achieving the projected revenue target is likely to be impacted.
  • Smoor to provide 5-6 influencer/UGC campaigns through the month to maintain creative freshness and support the increased September target.
  • August-generated B2B leads will continue to be nurtured and followed up to maximise their potential for September conversions.
  • We have suggested Smoor to strengthen the organic efforts through themed organic content for Cakes, particularly on affordability and emotive gifting themes.
