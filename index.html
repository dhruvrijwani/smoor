01   Executive Summary
May at a glance

May's primary objective was to achieve ₹28.29 lakhs in revenue across all three business verticals — B2C, B2B, and Experience Center. We delivered ₹25,65,951 in total revenue, achieving 90.7% of target.

Overall ROAS fell short by approximately 5.51% against the target benchmark. The primary gap came from the B2B segment, which generated ₹1,93,988 despite multiple optimisations across the month. The B2B shortfall of ₹5,39,348, combined with an Experience Center shortfall of ₹78,231, impacted the consolidated result.

On the positive side, B2C exceeded its revenue and ROAS targets, generating ₹3,10,996 above plan. Experience Center also surpassed its ROAS target. From a category standpoint, Cakes & Dessert Bars led by revenue, followed by Candy & Chocolate.

B2C advertising ran entirely through Google Ads this month, while B2B and Experience Center campaigns operated on Meta.

06   B2B Deep Dive
Corporate pipeline

B2B was the primary underperformer in May, falling ₹5.39 lakhs short of its revenue target despite generating 82 qualified leads.

82
Total leads generated in May
Approximately 70% of leads generated were qualified. With new campaign optimisations and structural changes implemented, lead quality improvement is expected in the coming months. CPL came in at ₹657 against ₹53,886 in spend.
70%
Estimated lead qualification rate
B2B organic revenue (₹1,02,000) outpaced paid revenue (₹91,988), signalling that pipeline closure depends more on sales follow-through than media investment. Campaign structural changes have been made — performance impact expected from June onward.
