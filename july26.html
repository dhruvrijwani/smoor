Executive Summary

  • July's overall revenue was ₹36,06,035 on ₹1,50,591 of ad spend, a 23.95x blended ROI.
  • Total spend came in almost exactly on the ₹1,50,000 plan, just 0.4% over. But the money moved around a lot within that. Experience Center spent ₹16,972 less than planned once it was clearly on track, and that budget went into B2C's Google campaigns instead.
  • B2C brought in ₹14,80,570 across Delhi NCR and Pan India, paid and organic together, at 17.68x ROI on ₹83,760 spent. Delhi NCR stayed steady at 1.14x. The extra Google spend paid off well, returning 8.20x.
  • B2B closed at ₹2,03,983 against a ₹9,60,000 target. The shortfall came down to lead quality: the native form test brought in nearly half of July's leads, and most of them weren't sales-ready. We're fixing this in August.
  • Experience Center revenue was ₹19,21,482 on ₹28,028 spend, a 68.56x ROI, ahead of June's 67.13x. Paid media's share of that revenue jumped to 26%, up from just 4% in June.

Overview Summary

What happened in each business vertical this month.

B2C: Delhi NCR

  • NCR's second month of testing closed almost exactly on its ₹35,000 plan (actual ₹35,267) and pushed revenue from ₹33,377 in June to ₹40,287 in July, nudging ROI from 1.09x to 1.14x.
  • Candy & Chocolate is still on top, but Food Gift Baskets jumped to #2, almost entirely from one product (Memoire Tin). That category barely showed up in June, so it's worth watching whether this is a one-off or NCR starting to skew toward corporate gifting.

B2C: Pan India (Google)

  • Once Experience Center was clearly tracking to target mid-month, budget got pulled off EC and pushed into the two Pan India Pmax campaigns. Spend landed at ₹48,493 against a ₹30,000 plan, a 62% overspend.
  • It paid off. Revenue came in at ₹3,97,878 against a planned ₹1,05,947, an 8.20x return on the extra spend. EC's underspend (₹16,972) and Google's overspend (₹18,493) are close enough to confirm this was a deliberate shift.

B2B

  • B2B closed at ₹2,03,983 against a ₹9,60,000 target. The gap came down to lead quality: the native form test alone supplied nearly half of July's leads, and a large share of them weren't sales-ready. We also tested new creative themes, including weekend gifting, which brought in a good number of leads early on, but these leads weren't a fit for bulk, corporate-style orders the way our usual creatives are. Once the team flagged that these leads weren't qualifying, we paused those themes and moved on to others. This was one of the main reasons B2B fell short this month, and we're addressing it in August.

Experience Center

  • EC revenue was ₹19,21,482 on ₹28,028 spend, a 68.56x ROI. Spend was basically flat versus June (₹28,218).
  • Paid media's share of EC revenue rose to 26% (₹4,94,481), up from just 4% in June, even though spend barely moved. The Google Search campaign is now genuinely converting, not just Meta like in June. Since EC was already ahead of target, we moved extra budget to B2C's Google campaigns instead of adding more here. That call paid off, since the reallocated spend returned 8.20x elsewhere.
05

August Plan

Planned activity for each vertical in August 2026.

B2C

  • August folds B2C into one ₹65,000 Google budget across three campaigns (Brand India, Pmax Signature Cakes, Sales Pmax Chocolates). There's no separate Delhi NCR Meta line this time, a change from July's split approach.
  • Focus shifts to high-AOV products to drive bulk orders: Celebration Pack Box of 14, Anniversary Hamper Large, Food Gift Baskets, and cakes and desserts, backed by UGC and influencer content.

B2B

  • Spend and target stay the same as July: ₹40,000 against ₹9,60,000. The focus in August is fixing lead quality by tightening targeting and reviewing the native form, so the leads coming in are actually sales-ready.

Experience Center

  • ₹45,000 planned, targeting ₹11,04,124 in revenue at a projected 24.54x ROI. EC runs out of Bangalore since it's tied to the physical experience center itself. No flagship event on the calendar like June's Smoorland this time, so the plan leans on steady organic momentum plus the paid contribution built up in July.
