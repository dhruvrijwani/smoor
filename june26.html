01

Executive Summary

  • Overall revenue for June was ₹40,19,924 against a tracked ad spend of ₹82,746, giving a blended ROI of 48.58x.
  • Ad spend was kept low on purpose this month, since June was largely a test month for the Delhi NCR region.
  • Delhi NCR generated ₹33,377 in revenue on ₹30,652 spend, a 1.09x ROAS. Its share of overall revenue is still around 2%.
  • B2B had its second-highest revenue month of the year at ₹9,12,449, with 38.22x overall ROI (10.49x on paid ads).
  • The Experience Center had its highest revenue in the last four months at ₹18,94,358, driven mainly by organic and event-led bookings.

04

Overview Summary

What happened in each business vertical this month.

B2C — Delhi NCR

  • June's main objective was to test the Delhi NCR market and check whether this new region, currently around 2% of overall revenue, can scale to an efficient ROI.
  • Delhi NCR's revenue contribution did not move much this month, but spend was also kept low, so it is too early to draw conclusions on long-term potential.
  • The strategy used a mix of Awareness and Sales campaigns to build visibility and drive conversions around relevant occasions and gifting moments.
  • Ad spend was ₹30,652 and revenue was ₹33,377, giving a 1.09x ROAS.

B2B

  • B2B recorded its second-highest revenue month of the year at ₹9,12,449. This is expected to keep improving as we get closer to the festive season.
  • Paid ads contributed ₹2,50,492 of B2B revenue at a 10.49x ROAS.

Experience Center

  • The Experience Center had its highest revenue in four months at ₹18,94,358, driven by Father's Day, Yoga Day, and large Smoorland events that brought in a high volume of bookings.
  • Paid ads added ₹81,501 at a 2.89x ROAS. This was affected by a delayed Google Ads launch during the month.
  • Influencer-led promotions accounted for a large share of revenue, especially for the Smoorland event, given its larger booking capacity.
05

July Plan

Planned activity for each vertical in July 2026.

B2C

  • Continue testing Delhi NCR while also targeting the broader pan-India audience to capture upcoming seasonal demand.

B2B

  • Test multiple communication themes: Fun Friday Office Celebrations, Monthly Appreciation Hampers, Employee of the Month Rewards, and a few others.
  • Explore integrating Conversions API (CAPI) with the existing CRM setup to give ad platforms better feedback signals and improve lead quality.
  • Additional optimisations are already live in B2B lead generation campaigns to improve audience quality.

Experience Center

  • Aim to sustain June's growth. No major Smoorland events are scheduled right now, so the team is exploring corporate events, birthday celebrations, and other experiential formats to keep bookings up.
